WHO WE ARE

Empowering people to lead change.

THAMANI is a women and youth focused, not-for-profit organization committed to inclusive and sustainable development.

ABOUT THAMANI

Cultivating human potential for lasting change.

THAMANI for Women and Youth Development Initiative is a Nigerian, grassroots-focused non-governmental organisation committed to advancing women and youth inclusion, civic participation, leadership, peacebuilding, education, gender justice, economic resilience and community development.

Established in 2020 and registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission as an Incorporated Trustee in 2023, THAMANI works primarily across Northern Nigeria in partnership with communities, government institutions, civil society organisations, youth networks, women-led groups, traditional and religious institutions, and development partners.

We seek to build empowered, inclusive and resilient communities where women and young people are active drivers of social change, accountable governance, peace and sustainable development.

OUR DIRECTION

What guides our work.

VISION

We envision a just and inclusive Nigeria where women and youth thrive, advancing empowerment, equity and participation.

MISSION

To strengthen underserved and marginalized communities through inclusive programs, advocacy and partnerships that promote equity, participation and sustainable development in Nigeria.

OUR STORY

From a shared commitment to community transformation.

Our work is grounded in the belief that lasting change is achieved when communities have the knowledge, platforms, resources and agency to identify and address the challenges affecting them.

Since its inception, THAMANI has worked at the intersection of grassroots mobilisation, capacity strengthening, policy advocacy, community organising and social innovation.

Using participatory and rights-based approaches, we strengthen community leadership, expand civic and economic opportunities, promote social cohesion and influence public policy. We place strong emphasis on locally owned solutions, evidence-based advocacy, digital engagement and approaches that can be sustained beyond individual projects.
